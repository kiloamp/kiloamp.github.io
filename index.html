Carlos Lagoa

Five lūstra and hours.

Barcelona-born, Mallorca-raised, usually somewhere between aircraft, odd little engineering projects, friends, sea air, and a notebook that gets opened at the wrong times.

Mallorca

Island gravity.

I was born in Barcelona, then moved to Mallorca when I was around four. The island somehow gets more interesting and more fun every year.

Early summer

Swim, bike, run, laugh.

We even did an Olympic Triathlon. Kudos Jan, Nero, and Simeon. The quiet TODO remains exactly where it belongs: beat them next year.

Aviation

The long pull of airplanes.

Since I was little I have been around planes. Family and friends were in the business, so aviation slowly became part of the background noise of life, then part of the foreground.

Coastline

All shapes and sizes.

It continues to be a big part of my life. Here is an ultralight along the coast of Mallorca, which is exactly the kind of sentence that makes staying on the island sound very reasonable.

United Kingdom

Cars, code, and test data.

I moved to the UK, got into tech and engineering, joined my university's Formula Student team, and wrote my final year thesis with them.

In between

Not everything was horsepower.

There were computers, pubs, long walks, cosy houses, quiet stretches, and friends. The UK years were very good at making things click internally.

Back to Spain

The CPL chapter.

I moved back to Spain and got my Commercial Pilot License. It happened around mid-Covid, which made the process lengthy, strange, and probably more character-building than anyone ordered.

Solo cross-country

The good bit.

Some schools suck, and testing style could be better. But the solo journeys were magic: a little aircraft, a plan, some weather, and enough room in the sky to learn about yourself.

Marple

Engineering for engineers.

I work at Marple, a Belgian startup building test data analysis software for engineers, mostly around cars and planes.

People

Team as weather system.

The people are unbelievably fun to be around, and the work lines up with where I would like to go next: flight test engineering.

Right now

Still flying.

I still do some occasional flying, recently completed my Airbus A320 type rating, and keep adding new ways to make the sky feel familiar.
